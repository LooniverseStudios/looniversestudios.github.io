Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Pre-release privacy notice

Monkey Fruit Rush is currently under development. This notice describes our intended privacy practices and will be updated to reflect the final application before its public release.

1. Introduction

LOONIVERSE Studios is committed to respecting the privacy of its players. This Privacy Policy concerns our mobile game, Monkey Fruit Rush, and its official website.

2. Information Collection

Monkey Fruit Rush is designed to be playable without creating a personal account or directly providing a name, email address, or phone number.

Game preferences and high scores may be stored locally on the player's device.

Third-party services integrated into the released game may process device information, IP addresses, advertising identifiers where permitted, diagnostic information, and application interaction data.

3. Advertising

Monkey Fruit Rush uses Google AdMob for rewarded advertising. Players may voluntarily watch advertisements to receive in-game rewards.

Our intended advertising configuration is designed to provide age-appropriate advertising and prevent personalized advertising for children.

Learn more about Google's privacy practices: Google Privacy Policy .

4. Children's Privacy

Monkey Fruit Rush is intended to be a family-friendly game for players aged six and above.

We do not intend to knowingly collect personal information directly from children without the authorization required by applicable law.

Before public release, we intend to implement appropriate safeguards for children, including age-appropriate advertising and restrictions on data processing.

Parents or legal guardians may contact us with questions or concerns regarding their children's privacy.

5. In-App Purchases

Future versions of Monkey Fruit Rush may offer optional in-app purchases through Google Play.

Payment processing will be handled by Google Play. LOONIVERSE Studios does not intend to directly collect payment card details.

Purchase-related information may be processed to verify transactions and deliver purchased in-game benefits.

6. Use of Information

Information may be processed for purposes such as:

  • Providing and maintaining game functionality.
  • Saving game preferences and high scores.
  • Displaying permitted advertisements.
  • Delivering and verifying in-game rewards.
  • Processing optional purchases.
  • Maintaining application security and reliability.
  • Responding to support and privacy requests.

7. Third-Party Services

Our application and website may rely on third-party services that process information under their own privacy policies.

  • Google and Google AdMob
  • Google Play Services
  • GitHub Pages

8. Data Retention and Security

Locally stored game information may be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application, subject to device backup settings.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to their applicable policies and legal obligations.

We aim to apply reasonable safeguards to information under our control.

9. Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, users and their legal guardians may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of personal information.

Privacy requests may be submitted using the contact information below.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our application, services, and legal obligations evolve. Updated versions will be published on this page.
